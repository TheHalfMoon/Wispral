# Wispral Community Conduct

Wispral aims to be a technically rigorous and welcoming open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, evidence, code, and designs rather than attacking people.
- Be precise about uncertainty and willing to correct mistakes.
- Respect different experience levels, accents, languages, accessibility needs, and development environments.
- Credit external work and contributors accurately.
- Keep security reports and sensitive data in appropriate private channels.
- Give actionable review feedback with enough context to improve the work.
- Disagree strongly when necessary without harassment, threats, discrimination, or humiliation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets/private data, impersonation, spam, and sustained bad-faith disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove disruptive content; reject contributions; restrict participation; or escalate to platform moderation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

Enforcement should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and focused on restoring a safe, productive technical environment.

## Scope

This standard applies to Wispral-managed repositories, issue/PR discussions, project community spaces, and interactions where a participant is representing the Wispral project.
