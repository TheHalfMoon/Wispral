# 0001. Proposed donor boundaries for the platform replan

Status: proposed (2026-10-21); research recommendation only, not ratified product authority.

## Context

Issue #122 authorizes source investigation and complete replanning, while the active constitution and 000G still close broader product implementation. The studies identify usable native dictation, studio, dialogue and authority mechanisms with incompatible runtimes and differing cancellation semantics.

## Decision proposed

Retain one Rust authority/provenance/resource Core. Prefer Handy capture/delivery, selective VoiceStudio services, Parlant dialogue with a brokered ToolService, optional Chatwoot inbox and selected Botpress connectors. Preserve incompatible speech engines as separately locked workers. Use one realtime turn controller per session. Keep ACP developer-agent control as an optional capability. Do not import whole security platforms, duplicate model managers or rewrite speech engines for language uniformity.

This ADR intentionally remains proposed because the founder requests a governance proposal without bypassing canonical authority. Exact source identities and alternatives are in the [transplant matrix](../../research/rea/2026-10-21/SOURCE_TRANSPLANT_MATRIX.md), and the [architecture replan](../strategy/WISPRAL_PLATFORM_ARCHITECTURE_REPLAN.md) states consequences and qualification.

## Consequences

Reuse preserves tested donor mechanisms and limits duplicate responsibility. It also creates process, schema, packaging and resource boundaries that require independent tests. No measured speech-quality winner exists. Revisit a lead when its qualified extraction fails, the release scope changes, or held-out results establish a better tradeoff. Adoption requires a dedicated amendment and applicable Grain; no earlier ADR or historical evidence is superseded by this proposal.
