## Candidate advantages to test, not market

P0: fail-closed target/tenant/consent boundaries and inspectable provenance can distinguish the platform only if adversarial runtime fixtures pass. P1: a useful local Arabic/Gulf/English path, resumable studio production and recoverable human handoff need held-out human/audio and OS evidence. P2: one common inventory schema with one implementation per owning runtime may improve consistency; cross-process sharing is deferred and no footprint reduction was measured. Cross-platform and mobile expansion must earn separate support cells.

No public vendor latency, language-count, compliance, savings or quality claim is adopted as an independent fact about behavior. Scope and source architecture support provisional build choices; production parity requires actual app/account/hardware trials and disclosed denominators.
