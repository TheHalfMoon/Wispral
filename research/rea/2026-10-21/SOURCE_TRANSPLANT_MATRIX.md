# Source transplant matrix

All destinations are proposed. No product source was transplanted in this research mission. COPY means retain a small qualified implementation with notices; ADAPT means preserve its mechanism and change a documented boundary; WRAP preserves a package API; OUT_OF_PROCESS preserves an incompatible service/runtime. REFERENCE_ONLY is not runtime reuse. The [machine-readable matrix](source-transplants.json) preserves dependencies, tests and risks; pillar studies explain execution paths.
